# Hi, I'm Anu

I'm a teacher with seven years in education, training, and assessment, now
building toward AI governance. I started independent AI governance projects
in May 2026 and earned the IAPP AI Governance Professional (AIGP)
certification in 2026.

## What I'm working on

My main repo is
[ai-governance-projects](https://github.com/anakha15307/ai-governance-projects):
17 projects plus 2 case studies covering safety evaluation, risk assessment,
and governance tooling, mapped to NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, and the EU AI
Act. Three flagships carry the most weight:

- **Laya governance experiments**: tested a 421M-parameter decision model as
  a safety gate, a calibrated confidence source, and a bias probe. 85%
  accuracy on 60 hand-built prompts, with Wilson confidence intervals, a
  rules baseline, and an honest statement that none of it is evidence of
  production readiness.
- **AI Governance Council**: three LLMs classified 8 synthetic EU AI Act
  cases, deliberated, and voted. The council reached 7/8 (87.5%). The
  honest finding: confidence scores had no signal, and free-tier
  reliability, not model quality, was the binding constraint.
- **AI governance playbook**: a 16-chapter operating manual with 11 fillable
  templates for running an AI governance program.

I also keep a [portfolio repo](https://github.com/anakha15307/ai-governance-portfolio)
with two full case studies: an LLM evaluation case study and a Khanmigo
risk assessment.
